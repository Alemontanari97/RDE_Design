\chapter{The GENO full-field plug twin}
\label{ch:genotwin}

Two programs written independently, in different languages,
checking each other on one physical problem is a stronger test than
anything either can run alone: a shared answer cannot come from a
shared mistake. This chapter runs that experiment on a \emph{plug}
(aerospike) nozzle --- one whose solid wall, the \emph{spike}, sits
under the exhaust while the outside of the jet is bounded by
nothing but the surrounding atmosphere. The independent Fortran
design code GENO~\cite{geno} designs such a spike; our solver takes
that shape as an ordinary given wall, recomputes the whole flow
around it, and the two flowfields are confronted number by number.
The step delivers the program's strongest external check --- and
one honest finding.

\section{The question}

The two-constraint GENO session (Chapter~\ref{ch:status},
Section~\ref{sec:designinputs}) left one named residual: the
\emph{full-field} comparison. Not a handful of summary quantities,
but our certified plug march run on the spike contour GENO's
RaoPlug module now cleanly produces, compared field against field
--- wall pressure station by station, velocities at GENO's own
interior points, and the integrated mass and thrust. This is the
plug counterpart of the bell nozzle's cross-code check of
Chapter~\ref{ch:referee}.

\section{How it works, in full}

\subsection{What you need to know first}

The solver advances a supersonic flow along the two families of
\emph{characteristics} --- the lines along which a disturbance can
travel in supersonic flow, tilted by the Mach angle on either side
of the flow direction (Chapter~\ref{ch:moc}; symbols in
Table~\ref{tab:notation}). Three more words are needed here.

A \emph{start line} (or Cauchy cut, after the initial-value problem
it poses) is a line drawn across the flow on which the complete
state --- position, velocity, pressure --- is supplied as data.
Given a start line and the walls, the equations determine
everything downstream of it and nothing upstream. Ours is a
vertical cut across GENO's finished field.

The \emph{initial-value line} (IVL) is the last characteristic of
GENO's own construction --- the outer edge of the region GENO
computes. Below it is GENO's design field; above it GENO has
nothing to say.

\emph{Truncation} appears here in two unrelated standard senses,
and the chapter needs both: cutting the spike short at a finite
length (hardware), and the error a finite-step scheme makes because
its steps are not infinitesimal (numerics). The finding below is
about the second.

\subsection{Two independent constructions}

GENO builds the spike \emph{backward}: it rotates the expansion fan
at the cowl lip in fixed angular steps ($0.05^{\circ}$), integrates
one characteristic per fan member with its own cells, places each
wall point by requiring that the mass flowing inside that
characteristic equal the mass the throat passes (the mass
termination, GENO's N-65), and cuts the spike at the point $D$
where that balance is met.

Our engine knows nothing of that construction. It is a general
axisymmetric march --- interior cells, prescribed-wall cells with
the multi-column foot search, the free-jet edge, each certified in
Chapter~\ref{ch:plug} --- and it treats GENO's contour as an
ordinary given wall, marching \emph{forward} from a start line.
Agreement is therefore evidence about the shared continuum problem,
not about shared code.

\subsection{The posing: all data GENO's own}

Nothing analytic is assumed. Even the gas is identified
\emph{from GENO's field}: the ratio of specific heats
$\gamma = 5.34782609/4.34782609$ (the recipe GENO's own thermo
backend implements, a gas of constant specific heat), the gas
constant $R_g$ from $p/(\rho T)$ at its points (scatter
$8.7\times10^{-9}$), and the field's own effective isentrope. That
last one matters: GENO's field actually sits at a chamber pressure
of $p_0 = 6.008172\times10^{6}$~Pa, \emph{not} the nominal
$6\times10^{6}$ --- a uniform offset of its construction, spread
$1.1\times10^{-4}$ --- and posing the nominal value would have
manufactured a difference that is not there.

The start line is a vertical cut at $x_0 = 0.15$ through GENO's own
first-phase characteristics; each crossing is interpolated on that
curve's own points and cross-checked against an independent
two-dimensional interpolation of the same field, agreeing to
$10^{-5}$. The wall is GENO's contour, with slopes
$\tan\theta_w$ measured from it (its tangency is consistent to
$1.6\times10^{-5}$).

Above the initial-value line lies a region GENO does not compute,
and our march needs something there, so a free-jet strip is posed,
blending continuously from the state at the IVL crossing to the
state on the last fan member. It cannot contaminate the comparison
--- a fact about supersonic flow, not a hope, since each point is
influenced only by what lies upstream inside its cone of
dependence: every characteristic arriving at the wall ahead of $D$
leaves the start line \emph{below} the IVL, and the IVL itself
lands exactly at $D$, the end of the wall. It was verified anyway:
replacing the blended strip by a uniform one changes the wall by
nothing.

\subsection{Thresholds: declared, with provenance}

Everywhere else in this program the pass band is \emph{derived}:
the same computation is run at two resolutions, the change measures
the scheme's own error, and the band is that change inflated by a
safety factor (Chapter~\ref{ch:method}). Here the levels are
instead \emph{declared} --- fixed in advance, with their provenance
--- deliberately, for the reason that is this step's central
finding below: the measured difference between the two codes sits
\emph{above} what either code's own step refinement suggests, so a
refinement-derived band would be the wrong ruler.

The declared levels come from the cross-code agreement already
established --- an independent integrator of the design curve,
built in the two-constraint session, matched GENO to three or four
significant digits --- re-measured here on the full field, with at
least a factor of two of margin on every statistic: wall pressure
$3\times10^{-3}$ (measured median $1.9\times10^{-3}$), settled
field $q\ 10^{-3}$ / $\theta\ 1.5\times10^{-3}$ (measured 95th
percentile $4.5\times10^{-4}$ / $5.3\times10^{-4}$), integrals
through the shared boundary $2.5\times10^{-3}$ (measured
$1.2\times10^{-3}$). What keeps declared numbers honest are the
\emph{rejectors} --- deliberately corrupted runs the suite must
fail (Chapter~\ref{ch:method}): they prove the instrument resolves
defects an order of magnitude above the gap it is measuring. One
check keeps fully derived bands: the gas identification is judged
against GENO's own measured self-consistency floors (total enthalpy
$2.0\times10^{-5}$, entropy $4.7\times10^{-6}R$, total pressure
$1.1\times10^{-4}$).

\section{What was built}

\carrier{validation/a1\_geno\_plug\_twin.py}: the reference
extractor (GENO run directory $\to$ \code{\_geno\_twin/ref.npz}:
wall, initial-value line, the 131{,}589 first-phase field points,
gas identification, floors), the start-line world builder, the
calibrated flux quadrature --- along GENO's full initial-value line
it reproduces GENO's printed mass flow $\dot m$ to
$2.4\times10^{-7}$ and thrust $F$ to $2.3\times10^{-7}$, settling a
question of definition on the way: GENO's $F$ is the
absolute-pressure momentum integral --- plus the six agreement
checks G-1--G-6 and the two rejectors R-1/R-2.

\section{Results of record: 8/8 PASS}

G-1, the gas: our tables reproduce GENO's pressure, temperature and
density at its own points from the local speed alone, within GENO's
measured floors (worst $9.9\times10^{-5}$ in $p$). G-2: all
25{,}034 cells Newton-certified at both resolutions. G-3, the wall:
pressure within $3\times10^{-3}$ at 100\% of stations (median
$1.9\times10^{-3}$, worst $2.5\times10^{-3}$). G-4, the interior:
past the start transient, 97.7\% of GENO's own points agree within
($q\ 10^{-3}$, $\theta\ 1.5\times10^{-3}$); medians
$3.8\times10^{-4}$ and $7.9\times10^{-5}$. G-5/G-6: mass and thrust
through the shared segment of the initial-value line agree to
$1.2\times10^{-3}$ and $1.1\times10^{-3}$. Both rejectors fire: a
gas falsified by $1\%$ ($\gamma\times1.01$) collapses the wall
agreement to 31\% of stations, a contour scaled by
$\times1.005$ collapses it to 0\%.

\section{The finding: a measured, attributed-open cross-code gap}
\label{sec:twingap}

The twin certifies three-to-four-digit agreement --- and the
residual difference is itself a characterized result, not a
leftover. It has two parts. Near the start line, a transient: at
mid heights the flow angle differs by up to $1.1\times10^{-2}$~rad,
decaying by $x \approx 0.35$, unchanged when the resolution is
changed, and moving when the start line moves --- the signature of
ingesting a start line drawn through the strong flow curvature near
the lip. It washes out. What remains is a settled \emph{uniform
drift}: our speed runs $+4\times10^{-4}$ relative high across the
whole column, and that excess grows linearly with the distance
marched (Figure~\ref{fig:genotwin}c).

The forensics excluded, by measurement, every accessible cause.
\emph{The gas}: both sides are analytically equivalent to about
$10^{-8}$. \emph{The wall geometry and its tangency}:
$1.6\times10^{-5}$. \emph{The start-line data}: agreed to $10^{-5}$
by two independent interpolation routes; refining the polyline
resolution from $10^{-4}$ to $5\times10^{-6}$ leaves the drift
unchanged. \emph{The strip above the initial-value line}: replacing
the blend by a uniform state changes nothing, and the cone of
dependence forbids it from reaching the wall anyway.
\emph{GENO's discretization}: a diagnostic GENO build with the fan
step \emph{and} the curve step both halved moved GENO's wall by
$3.5\times10^{-6}$, so GENO is step-converged (the sources were
then reverted and the binary restored, md5-verified).
\emph{Our own resolution}: halving each of our two step counts
moves the deviation by less than 10\% of itself.

Both fields satisfy the same trapezoidal march relations to
$10^{-8}$--$10^{-9}$ (an audit of the residuals run on
\emph{both} meshes). That proves the two discrete systems are
algebraically equivalent --- and it also proves that such an audit
is \emph{tautological} as an accuracy check: each code solves its
own relations exactly, so a residual audit can never see a slowly
accumulating bias. Worth stating plainly, because it looks like a
strong check and is not. Finally, a control march with the
axisymmetric source term switched off sizes the drift: it is
$0.5\%$ of what that term contributes to the wall velocity (which
is itself $-5.4\%$ of $q$).

Attribution stays honestly \emph{open}. The candidate mechanisms
live precisely where refinement does not vary anything --- on
GENO's side the bookkeeping of re-interpolating during its backward
march, on ours the interpolation along the chord found by the foot
search --- and from outside, at this level, they cannot be told
apart. The owner-side question is filed with GENO. For this program
the operational consequence is the one already adopted: cross-code
comparisons with GENO carry the measured three-to-four-digit
agreement level --- far above the truncation error of either code,
far below anything the optimizer's gradients are sensitive to ---
and the rejectors keep the instrument honest at that level.
