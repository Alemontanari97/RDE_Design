\chapter{Free-vortex swirl}
\label{ch:swirl}

Everything so far assumed the gas moves in a plane containing the
axis: forward and outward, never around. A rotating detonation
engine (RDE) --- a combustor in which a detonation wave runs
continuously around an annular chamber --- does not oblige: its
exhaust comes out \emph{spinning}. This chapter adds that spin to
the solver in the one form that costs no new assumptions, shows by
proof and by measurement that the whole certified machinery
survives it, and states plainly what the extension does and does
not model.

\section{The question}

Because the detonation wave sweeps around the chamber, it drags the
gas with it: the exhaust carries an \emph{azimuthal velocity} $w$
--- the component of velocity that goes \emph{around} the axis,
perpendicular to the plane through the axis in which the solver
works (that plane is called the \emph{meridional} plane, and $q_m$
denotes the speed in it). The theory corpus guarantees (its
statement N6-2) that for one particular swirl profile --- the
\emph{free vortex} --- the entire certified march structure
survives untouched, and that anything beyond it is a class of flow
this method cannot represent at all, and which belongs to the
CFD-adjoint track.

A free vortex is the swirl of a bathtub drain far from its core:
the product of radius and azimuthal velocity, $\Gamma = y\,w$, is
\emph{one and the same number everywhere in the flow}. That product
is called the \emph{circulation}; it measures how much the gas
circles the axis, and physically it is the angular momentum per
unit mass. So the swirl is fastest near the axis and dies away
outward, and it is fixed by a single scalar $\Gamma$.

The question of this chapter: can the engine carry swirl, with the
guarantee \emph{measured} rather than assumed --- and with the
swirl turned off ($\Gamma \to 0$) recovering the certified
swirl-free march exactly?

\section{How it works, in full}

\subsection{Why free vortex is the one free extension}

Three exact facts make the free vortex special.

First, the equation for momentum around the axis says that
$y\,w$ is carried unchanged along each streamline. If it is one
constant \emph{everywhere}, that equation is satisfied identically
and $w = \Gamma/y$ is known from position alone --- no new unknown
enters the march.

Second, the flow stays \emph{irrotational}: fluid parcels are
carried along and deformed, but they do not spin about their own
centres. This is the property the whole solver rests on --- it is
what makes the flow describable by the two characteristic families
of Chapter~\ref{ch:moc}; a flow whose parcels do spin is called
\emph{rotational}, and for it the method of characteristics no
longer applies. That is exactly what lies beyond the free vortex.
Crocco's theorem, which ties
vorticity to gradients of total enthalpy and entropy, leaves only
an azimuthal component possible when both are uniform, and the
condition $V \times \omega = 0$ then forces even that to vanish. So
the meridional flow stays irrotational, and the certified
two-characteristic structure is not an approximation here but
exact.

Third, working the gas-dynamic equations through with
$w = \Gamma/y$ shows that the meridional problem changes in exactly
two places and nowhere else:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{the state}: the kinetic energy of the spin joins the
  energy budget, $h = h_0 - q_m^2/2 - \Gamma^2/(2y^2)$, so the
  thermodynamic state now depends on the radius $y$ as well as on
  the meridional speed $q_m$;
\item \textbf{the source}: spinning gas must be held in by a
  pressure gradient pointing inward, and that centrifugal
  contribution adds to the term that axisymmetric flow already
  carries --- the \emph{axisymmetric source term}, the extra piece
  in the compatibility relations that accounts for the flow area
  opening out as $y$ grows, absent in planar flow. It becomes
  $c^2 v/y \;\to\; (c^2 + \Gamma^2/y^2)\,v/y$.
\end{itemize}
The characteristic directions keep their form, now at the
\emph{meridional} Mach angle, and the compatibility coefficients in
$(u, v)$ are untouched.

\subsection{The implementation is two changes, and only two}

The state change costs nothing new. With the augmented speed
$q_{\rm eff} = \sqrt{q_m^2 + \Gamma^2/y^2}$ the energy closure
reads $h = h_0 - q_{\rm eff}^2/2$, exactly the swirl-free form, so
the certified thermodynamic machinery applies verbatim to
$q_{\rm eff}$ and only the Mach number is rebuilt from the
meridional speed. The free jet edge --- the boundary where the
exhaust meets the atmosphere, on which the pressure is prescribed
--- keeps its certified parameterization: $p = p_a$ fixes the
\emph{total} speed, the same scalar as without swirl, so the edge's
meridional speed follows an explicit law,
$q_m(y) = \sqrt{q_{p_a}^2 - \Gamma^2/y^2}$.

The three cell types are mirrored with the swirl state and source
and then run through the \emph{unchanged} certified march driver
through an additive seam, whose defaults reproduce the certified
path exactly --- both plug-march suites were re-run after the seam
and still PASS.

One numerical lesson surfaced and is fixed in the swirl cells. The
cell's small nonlinear system mixes rows of very different size:
geometry rows of order one against compatibility rows of order
$u^2$. The damped Newton iteration --- which accepts a step only if
a norm of the residual decreases --- can then stall one step short
of the root, because the step that fixes the position trades a tiny
increase in the compatibility rows against a geometry correction of
order $10^{-7}$, and the damping rejects it forever. Dividing the
compatibility rows by a constant reference speed --- a pure
rescaling of the equations that moves no solution --- restores
certified convergence (worst cell $0.009$ of tolerance).

\subsection{What this is, and is not: the spiral phase pattern}
\label{sec:spiral}

The free vortex models the \emph{time-mean} azimuthal velocity of
the exhaust. It does not model the \emph{spiraling of the phase
pattern}: in a real RDE the surfaces of constant phase are helices
--- at one instant, different axial stations (and different
azimuths) are at different points of the cycle, because the wave
rotates while the flow transits. The cycle layer of
Chapter~\ref{ch:cycle} is deliberately \emph{quasi-steady}: each
phase fills the whole nozzle as a steady flow, and the cycle is an
average over the phases with no clock. Two distinct physical
effects are set aside by that closure. First,
\emph{unsteadiness within a meridional ray}: the phase seen at
station $x$ lags the injector phase by the transit time, and if the
cycle period is comparable to the time gas spends in the nozzle,
the flow never equilibrates to any phase --- the quasi-steady model
is the limit of vanishing reduced frequency, and for representative
RDE numbers (kHz rotation against sub-millisecond residence) that
frequency is of order one, so this is a real modeling limit,
stated. Second, \emph{azimuthal coupling}: the helical arrangement
tilts wave fronts out of the meridional plane --- genuinely
three-dimensional gasdynamics that no per-phase axisymmetric model
can see. Both effects carry a partial mitigation and a clean escape
route. The mitigation: if meridional rays were decoupled and
quasi-steady, the \emph{time average} at each station would equal
the average over phases regardless of how the phases are arranged
in space --- the spiral reshuffles \emph{when}, not \emph{what};
the cycle-averaged objective is insensitive to the arrangement at
that order. The escape route: both departures live exactly where
the corpus already points --- the unsteady three-dimensional CFD
track, whose simulations contain the spiral natively and for which
this engine is the oracle bank.

\subsection{The worlds}

Two test worlds, both on the real-gas tables, both under a strong
swirl ($w/u = 0.35$ at the inner radius).

The \emph{radial-equilibrium duct} is an exact known answer. In a
straight annular duct the swirling solution has \emph{uniform}
axial speed and no radial velocity at all: differentiating the
energy closure along $y$ and subtracting the radial balance between
pressure gradient and centrifugal force,
$\mathrm{d}p/\mathrm{d}y = \rho\Gamma^2/y^3$, gives
$\mathrm{d}u/\mathrm{d}y = 0$ exactly --- while the entire radial
structure of pressure, temperature, sound speed and Mach number is
carried by the swirl term (Figure~\ref{fig:swirl}a). The march must
\emph{preserve} that state, and any drift is pure error.

The \emph{expansion world} drops the wall smoothly under the same
stream, so the flow is genuinely two-dimensional ($v \neq 0$) and
the centrifugal source actually acts. The duct alone cannot see
that source, because there $v = 0$ multiplies it away.

\section{What was built}

\carrier{validation/a1\_swirl\_march.py}: the swirl state
(augmented speed) and its coefficients, the three mirrored cell
processes with the row scaling, swirl-aware flux and wall-push
integrals, the two worlds, and the suite below. The certified
driver gained the additive seam (\code{cells=}, \code{q\_edge=});
its own oracle and axisymmetric suites re-ran 6/6 and 5/5 after the
change.

\section{Results of record: 9/9 PASS}

W-1, the switch-off regression: at zero circulation the swirl cells
reproduce the certified cells' march to $2.1\times10^{-13}$,
against a band of $10^{-9}$ relative --- the certified per-cell
Newton tolerance, meaning every cell's nonlinear system was solved
to a residual inside a derived band. The only arithmetic difference
is the augmented-speed square root.

W-2, the exact duct: all cells Newton-certified (worst $0.009$ of
tolerance), and the equilibrium is preserved to machine zero ---
axial-speed drift $1.6\times10^{-15}$, $|v|/u\ 2.3\times10^{-15}$,
wall pressure $4.9\times10^{-15}$, edge position exact --- inside
Richardson bands, the allowed errors obtained by re-running with
the station spacing halved and letting the scheme measure its own
truncation error (Chapter~\ref{ch:method}).

W-3, the expansion world: mass closes to $7.8$ out of
$4.3\times10^{4}$ (band $16$), and the momentum theorem in the
axial direction closes within band --- unchanged by swirl, because
the centrifugal force is radial and the momentum around the axis
decouples from it; checked, not assumed.

W-4, the two live controls: switching the swirl off moves the wall
speed $8.6\times$ beyond the pair's own band, and dropping
\emph{only} the centrifugal source while keeping $\Gamma$ in the
state moves it $12\times$ beyond --- so the new term is genuinely
wired in and genuinely material (Figure~\ref{fig:swirl}b). R-1, the
rejector: cells fed a corrupted circulation ($\Gamma^2 \times
1.10$) but marched on true equilibrium data leave the preservation
bands by twelve orders of magnitude --- caught, as required.
