non-reproduction caveat: *material reproduced for discussion, not re-derived*. Each crop below
is shown in the deck only with attribution to the row's citation; no claim in the deck is
derived from the pixels — numbers quoted next to a crop must trace to the paper text/table
cited here.

retro-audit Blocco 2 criterio (e) verifica file-vs-manifest-vs-slide.
