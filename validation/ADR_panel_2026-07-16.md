  desc: For the RDE aerospike the exit is a projected annulus, not a circle: cowl lip at R_lip = R_bar + gap/2 (147.5 mm at 10 kN; 47.5 mm at 600 N nozzled). Three computed consequences: (i) the OD check is 2*R_lip (295 mm <= 320 OK) — the circular D_e = 209.4 mm is meaningless for this config; (ii) the geometric expansion available is pi*R_lip^2/A_t = 21.1 >= eps* = 10.64, so the paper's Eq.-11/12 exit-area limit does not bind (consistent with how the repo already implements the spike); (iii) plug length scales with the PHYSICAL lip: L_ideal = 438 mm vs 311 mm circular-equivalent — the honest annular treatment makes the length constraint MORE binding and is what decides feasibility in BOTH examples (10 kN: fails all truncations; 600 N: 25.1 vs 20 mm). For the bell config, the annulus->circular-throat transition adds unmodeled length (declare). This is a formula choice inside O-a/O-c, not a separate mechanism — but it is the single largest number-mover in the whole option space (+41% on plug length at 10 kN).
  refs: Angelino 1964 (annular plug geometry from the lip); Hagemann et al. 1998 (annular/toroidal plug configurations, J-2T); Stechmann JSR 2019 Eqs. 11-12 define the RDE spike by its exit-area limit — the annular reading is the paper's own framing; Harroun, Heister & Ruf, JPP 2021 (actual RDRE aerospike geometry: centerbody plug aft of the annulus).
  quantified: Pure geometry, no Isp path touched. Swings the 10 kN plug estimate 311->438 mm ideal (62-124 -> 88-175 mm truncated) and flips the 20%-truncation verdict from 'fits by 18 mm' (circular) to 'violates by 8 mm' (physical). Dossier judgment aid: presenting BOTH as a declared band (like the lambda band) is the honest posture; silently picking circular-equivalent is the only way the current headline 'passes', and it is the less physical choice.
