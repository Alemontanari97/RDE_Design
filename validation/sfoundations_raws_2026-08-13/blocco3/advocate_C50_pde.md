### T3 — The exactness theorem: the "conservative" L1 bound is the

it addresses S1′'s APPLICATION, which is precisely what D1 exists to
adjudicate. S1′ (adopted): "the sensitivity constant must be the
actual dual pairing against the perturbation class (sharp), not a
worst-case-per-unit-mass envelope." Elementary fact (standard
robust-optimization duality, delta-census §6 [ABS]; one line):

  sup { |⟨Λ, δ⟩| : ‖δ‖_L1(A) ≤ ε } = ‖Λ‖_L∞(A) · ε,

attained (in the limit) by concentrating the perturbation where |Λ|

certifies an L1 radius and nothing finer — the "worst-case-per-unit-
mass envelope" and the "actual dual pairing against the perturbation
class" are THE SAME NUMBER. The envelope is not conservative FOR THE
BALL; it is tight. The distinction S1′ draws is non-empty only when
the certified class is strictly smaller than the L1 ball — a
G-weighted ellipsoid, a covariance structure, per-component bands.

RICHER PROVENANCE INFORMATION, which is exactly what [DATA]'s own
grade ladder U-a → U-b → U-c is for (contract_blind_data.md Def 7.2,
cited via PANEL_C50 §2.3). Therefore:

  sharpness is a property of the certified information GRADE,
  not of the datum-space metric.

## §3 EXPLICIT ANSWERS TO THE STRONGEST POINTS AGAINST L1

**(P-1) "The L1 sensitivity constant is worst-case-per-unit-mass,
blind to WHERE thrust is sensitive" (panel §3.1 case-against; core
of R3C50-1/S1′).** Answered by T3 in full: the constant is EXACTLY
the sup of the true first-order response over the certified ball —
tightness, not blindness. What the objection actually asserts is
that the L1 ball contains perturbations the physical error process
would never produce (all error mass concentrated at the most
Λ-sensitive point). Granted — but excluding them requires CERTIFIED
knowledge that they cannot occur, and that knowledge is a provenance
grade (U-b structure), not a metric. Until a delivery certifies it,
the sharp interval computed in a G-weighted geometry is not a
certificate; it is an assumption wearing one. The tie rule then
does exactly its job: it reports the honest tie that the certified
information supports. The moment a delivery certifies more, the
U slot carries it and the intervals sharpen — same contract, no
metric change.

**(P-2) "Conservatism predictably drives the tie rule vacuous"
(S1′ tail; the well-posedness reading the wave judge legitimated).**
Three answers, in descending strength. (a) Empirical status: the
vacuity is a PREDICTION about magnitudes (‖Λ‖_L∞·ε vs typical
inter-design ΔJ) for deliveries that do not exist; the panel's own
materiality clause (immaterial-at-constants today, NO-ATTACK) cuts
both ways. I pin it as my own falsifier FL-3 (§5) rather than
accept it as a premise. (b) Route completeness: C7.2's sampled
[min,max] route (:622-624) is available to L1-solo and is sharp for
the ball; vacuity-by-envelope is therefore at worst a COST problem
(sampling expense), and cost problems are priced, not architected
around, in this program. (c) Structural mitigation already in hand:
the product form removes the front-jitter mass from the envelope
term (UC-5), which is precisely the term the panel's own analysis
identifies as dominant for this data class. After (c), the residual
envelope conservatism concerns the SMOOTH field part, where Λ is
the trace of a smooth-region adjoint and its L∞/weighted gap is a

**(P-4) "The sensitivity dual for front perturbations is not an L2
