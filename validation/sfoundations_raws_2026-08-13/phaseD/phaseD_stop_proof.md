## §2 The equivariance half [T-T0P-E]

### §2.1 The group and its action

The problem's symmetry group is G_full := SO(2)_theta x R_t, acting
on fields by rigid rotation about e_x combined with time translation.
For phi in R and tau in R define, on any field q = (rho, u, S-etc.)
over Omega x R:

    (g_{phi,tau} q)(x, X_perp, t) := R_phi [ q(x, R_{-phi} X_perp,
                                              t - tau) ],          (ACT)

where X_perp = (y, z), R_phi is the rotation by phi of the transverse
plane, and R_phi[.] acts on the state componentwise: scalars
(rho, S, p, e) unchanged, the velocity vector by (v, w) ->
R_phi (v, w), u unchanged. In cylindrical components
(rho, u_x, u_r, u_theta, S) the action is the pure shift
(theta, t) -> (theta - phi, t - tau), componentwise.

The HELICAL SUBGROUP selected by the data speed OM is the
one-parameter group

    g_tau := g_{OM tau, tau},   tau in R,                        (HEL)

together with the discrete rotation rho_n := g_{2pi/n, 0}.

[L-EQV1] LEMMA (rotation equivariance of Euler). THEOREM. Let R be a
rotation about e_x and q a weak solution of (EU) on Omega x R (H1).
Then q^R defined by (ACT) with tau = 0 is a weak solution of (EU) on
(R Omega) x R = Omega x R.
PROOF. Frame-indifference by direct substitution in the weak form.
Let phi_test be a test function and phi_test^R its rotation. The
fluxes of (EU) transform covariantly under the orthogonal map
R with det R = 1: mass and energy components are scalars,
momentum components a vector; explicitly, writing
F(W) . grad phi := F_x d_x phi + F_y d_y phi + F_z d_z phi, one has
for every state:  F(R W) . grad(phi_test o R^{-1})  evaluated at
R X  equals  R applied to [ F(W) . grad phi_test ] at X in the sense
that the scalar mass/energy rows coincide and the momentum rows are
the R-image — an elementary computation from the dyadic structure
rho u (x) u + p Id of the momentum flux (R (rho u (x) u + p Id) R^T
= rho (Ru)(x)(Ru) + p Id) and rho u H -> rho (Ru) H for the energy
row. The change of variables X -> R X in each integral of the weak
form (|det R| = 1) then maps the weak formulation for q exactly onto
the weak formulation for q^R. The EOS enters only through
p = p(rho, S), a rotational scalar: ABSTRACT EOS, gamma(T) included.
QED.
