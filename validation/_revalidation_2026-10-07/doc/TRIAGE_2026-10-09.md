# Registry triage after the revalidation (2026-10-09)

Consumer: the program owner, deciding what of the registry survives the 2026-10-07/08 revalidation of the rde-nozzle
optimiser. Exit criteria: every carrier the claims lint flags has a decision, a reason in one line, and (where it
survives) evidence run with the code that will be committed; nothing is committed without the owner's go.

## The request and the decision of record

Owner, 2026-10-09: triage the carriers partly redone and not redone by the revalidation, say why each should be
redone and whether it is needed, otherwise discard it; the redone ones are replaced by the new, re-certified tests.
Owner, after the proposal: "procedi con l'ordine e le priorita' da te date. Per il commit finale aspetta il mio nulla
osta." Order of work: model tests re-run with the committed defaults, a rejector in every test (rule R5), the old
verifiers that survive re-run, registry, commit (on the owner's go only).

## Criterion

A carrier stays alive when the tool uses it for the decisive comparison (the plug designed with the 12 phases
against the plug designed at the mean state; the base on a dedicated row; CFD as the final judge) or when it carries
a theorem of the paper, and no new test covers it. A carrier whose purpose a revalidation test now covers keeps its
ID and points to that test. Everything else is retired.

## Registry mechanics (why nothing is deleted)

The 37 IDs that leave the active record (15 re-pointed, 22 retired) are cited 182 times in brackets in docs/*.md, 73
of them in the append-only PROGRESS_ARCHIVE; the orphan lint (claims lint (e)) forbids an unknown cited ID. So:
- re-pointed carriers keep their ID; scope, statement, proof and stamp are replaced; the former record stays in the
  scope as "RECORD BEFORE THE REVALIDATION (HISTORY)";
- retired carriers keep their entry with the stamp `fail=2026-10-09` and a statement opening "RETIRED 2026-10-09",
  owned by the OPEN finding `revalidation:carriers-retired-2026-10-09` (the lint's channel for a record nobody vouches
  for); nothing is re-run;
- theory and directive rows citing them are re-pointed: T-LEMB and DIR-THERMOTAB gain the new carriers; DIR-REOB and
  DIR-RKG keep their IDs (now re-obtention tests and the agnostic driver).

## Day-granular staleness (read before committing)

The lint compares the DAY of the last commit touching a carrier's import closure with the carrier's pass date. Every
proof was re-run on 2026-10-09 with the code to be committed: the model tests, the old verifiers, and the eight slide
readings t17 WITHOUT their ladder cache (identical to the readings of 2026-10-08 value by value, so the slides do not
change). A commit on 2026-10-09 needs nothing more. A commit on a later day makes every carrier stale again: on that
day re-run the proofs (about 45 minutes in two waves inside the 12-thread cap: model tests and old verifiers, then the
readings), stamp, commit the stamps.
