"opportunamente rivalidate, qualora potessero migliorare la convergenza e la performance". Exit criteria: every change

## What of driver step 2 applies to the certified driver (read before any code)

Driver step 2 (local branch `driver-step2-2026-10-05`, commit 5f6f920, never merged; PROGRESS_2026-10-05_F3TWIN.md sec. 3,
4, 8) patches the former driver `run_trsqp` (segmented trust-constr). The certified driver since 2026-10-08 is
`a1_qntr` (`PSPL_DRIVER=qntr`, a1_plug_spline_opt.py), which the former driver now reaches only with `PSPL_DRIVER=legacy`.

| step 2 | the defect it fixed (2026-10-05) | a1_qntr | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| PSPL_SAFE | trust-constr returns its LAST iterate (14 of 40 trials worse than the base) | the trial is the model's argmax (exact, or SLSQP on the model) | does not apply |
| PSPL_TRCAP | the solver's step 23 times the radius | the step is inside the radius by construction | does not apply |
| PSPL_WARMBFGS | BFGS restarted from the identity at every segment | one damped BFGS for the whole walk (a1_twowall calls the driver once per walk) | already there |
| PSPL_BARRIER | the interior point's barrier | no interior point | does not apply |
| PSPL_TRGROW=k | radius x1.5 even far from the optimum | x2 when rho > 3/4 on the boundary | testable, little room (walks land in 4-14 trials) |
| lin_cons | general linear rows (pinned end, envelope) for smooth walls | bounds and the class row only | a capability; reopens O1 (owner's call) |

Where the time goes (the revalidation's records out/t17_*.json, runs of 2026-10-08): on the plug posings the two secant
Hessians are 85-94 percent of a walk (methane C0 5419 s of 6063; Rao 1961 4256-5990 s per walk); on the bells 12-19
percent. Step 2 has no lever there. Two levers that change the time and nothing else were therefore done instead
(owner, 2026-10-23: "vai").

## 1. QNTR_WORKERS: the secant Hessian's columns on workers (new)

  caller's module is imported, so jax's precision is set first; the parent's `__main__` is not re-imported by the workers,
  since a harness script run as `__main__` would run again in each of them); `Walk(..., pool=)`; `Walk.secant` takes the
  2n gradients from the pool and assembles the columns exactly as the serial loop. `a1_plug_spline_opt.run_trsqp` builds
