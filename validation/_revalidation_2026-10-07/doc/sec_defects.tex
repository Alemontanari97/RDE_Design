anti-aligned ($\cos=-0.71$) every segment returned a worse point, for every barrier and scaling tried. & The walk stalls
$5\times10^{-5}$ below the optimum (planar case); base-dependent (the annular case reached the optimum).\\\midrule

ill-conditioned (best step along $\nabla J$ $2.4\times10^{-4}$) and 6-iteration segments overshoot the frozen replay's

One \texttt{trust-constr} step per segment and a floor of $10^{-6}$ --- not a production setting --- closed the gap from
both starts, at about 2 hours per walk for 9 knots.\\\midrule

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textbf{Findings on GENO} (reference code, not modified)}\\
6 & Rao's input $\theta_i$ is not the flow angle at the lip: GENO places $(M_E,\theta_E)$ by \emph{one} midpoint step of

is at $2\theta_E-\theta_i$. & Inputs must be chosen by emulating that step (done here exactly: GENO's own
$\theta_i=35.33373^\circ$ for Rao's member is reproduced).\\
7 & Rao cannot start from a user's characteristic: its inflow is an output. & The model must take GENO's data.\\

$7\times10^{-4}$ rad near $D$; resolution fixed in the code. & Sets GENO's error floor in \S\ref{s:t06}.\\

\plain{The defects in plain words}{%
\textbf{1.} On very steep plugs a small equation-solving step inside the flow computation sometimes stops one step short:
the result is right but gets flagged as unreliable and is thrown away. A built-in option fixes it.
\textbf{2.} If the starting shape breaks the ``no folded flow'' rule, the optimiser has no working way to first repair it:
it simply does not move.
\textbf{3.} With the ``no folded flow'' rule switched on, the external optimisation library can propose a worse shape at
some starting points; the walk then gets stuck below the optimum.
\textbf{4.} The stopping rule says ``finished'' when moves become small; on a narrow ridge (Rao 1961) that happens before
the top. Short planned steps fix it, at a cost in time.
\textbf{5.} (Physics, not a bug.) At low chamber pressure a plug designed for the mean state over-expands the flow; shocks
must form, and a shock-free model has no answer there.}

\textbf{Do the RDE plugs live in the bad regime?} Defects 1 and 4 grow as the $C^+$ columns at the wall flatten,
$\theta_\mathrm{wall}+\mu\to0$. Smallest value along each designed plug (\texttt{t10c\_steepness}): planar ideal plug
$+19.5^\circ$, Rao methane plug (lip Mach 1.556, the RDE-like case) $+5.5^\circ$, annular nozzle $+4.7^\circ$, axial exit
$+0.13^\circ$, Rao 1961 $-3.3^\circ$. The default walk stopped short only on Rao 1961, the only plug where the value
is negative (the columns descend); the axial exit, at $+0.13^\circ$, converged. The RDE-like plug sits well away from it.
