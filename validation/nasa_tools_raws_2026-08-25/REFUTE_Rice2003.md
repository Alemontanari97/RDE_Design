## C. Report-internal defects the digest transcribed without flagging (method-relevant; affects connection C1)

The digest lists eq. 35 and two numbering slips as "report-internal defects". Two further defects exist in the 3-D equations and are more consequential because the digest presents eqs. 15/29–33 and 37–40 as a "complete worked example" for an F6 reference-plane march.

**C-1. Eq. 15 / eq. 29 (pp. 25, 29) — ψ coefficient printed as cosθ_i sinθ_i, should almost certainly be cosθ_i sinδ_i.**
Refuter derivation from the report's own geometry: with q̂ = (sinθ, cosθ sinψ, cosθ cosψ) (eqs. 12–14), the unit vectors ê₁ = ∂q̂/∂θ = (cosθ, −sinθ sinψ, −sinθ cosψ) (lies in the plane of q and x) and ê₂ = (∂q̂/∂ψ)/cosθ = (0, cosψ, −sinψ) span the plane normal to q. The ray direction in eqs. 9–11 is exactly cosβ q̂ + sinβ (cosδ ê₁ + sinδ ê₂) — verified component-by-component against the printed eqs. 9, 10, 11. The N-direction in eqs. 31–33 is exactly −sinδ ê₁ + cosδ ê₂ — verified against the printed eqs. 31, 32, 33. Consistently, the ∂/∂N bracket of eq. 15 carries (cosθ cosδ ∂ψ/∂N − sinδ ∂θ/∂N) = (dq̂·N-pattern). The velocity-direction change projected on the transverse ray direction is then (cosδ ê₁ + sinδ ê₂)·dq̂ = cosδ dθ + **cosθ sinδ** dψ. The printed cosθ sinθ (ψ₂ − ψ_i) makes the ψ coefficient δ-independent and identically zero at θ = 0 — for the report's own test cases (θ = 0 inflow, θ ≈ 0 in the symmetry plane) the 3×3 system for (P₂, θ₂, ψ₂) would be rank-deficient in ψ₂, yet the tool ran. Verdict: high-likelihood typo (sinθ for sinδ) in the report; the code presumably implements sinδ. Must be checked against Armstrong AEDC-TR-78-68 and/or the repo source before any equation from the digest's §4.1 is reused. Sign of the sinδ term depends on δ orientation convention; the functional form does not.

**C-2. Eq. 38 (p. 31) — index inconsistency in the right-hand side.**
Eqs. 39 and 40 fix the labelling of the wall normal as n₁ = z-component, n₂ = x-component, n₃ = y-component (eq. 40 n₁cosψ + n₂tanθ + n₃sinψ = 0 is n·q̂ = 0 / cosθ under that labelling; eq. 39 is cosδ₄ = n̂·ê₁ under the same labelling). With that labelling the plane through P₁ spanned by n and q̂ has normal m = n × q̂ with m_x = n₃cosθcosψ − n₁cosθsinψ (matches printed), m_y = n₁sinθ − n₂cosθcosψ (matches printed), m_z = n₂cosθsinψ − n₃sinθ, i.e. RHS should be (n₃ sinθ − **n₂** cosθ sinψ) dz; printed RHS is (n₃ sinθ − **n₁** cosθ sinψ) dz. Verdict: candidate typo (n₁ → n₂), moderate confidence (depends on the labelling inferred from eqs. 39–40; the report never states which component each n_k is — Nomenclature p. 42 says only "n Normal surface coefficient"). Same check-against-code requirement as C-1.

**C-3. Nomenclature vs eqs. 12–14.** p. 42 defines θ as "Flow angle ... w/rt the x-z plane" and ψ "w/rt the y-z plane", but eq. 12 u = q sinθ makes θ the elevation out of the y-z plane toward x (and ψ the azimuth in the y-z plane). Ambiguous wording, not necessarily wrong; any reimplementation should take the angles from eqs. 12–14, not from the Nomenclature.
