## Omissions (method-relevant content in the report, absent from the digest)

- **Outer iteration of the fully-coupled model on entrance Mach / throat total pressure.** Output pp. 43 and 67 print "nozzle entrance Mach number is ... inviscid flow model", then "nozzle throat total pressure is ..., pass number 1, in full model", "nozzle entrance Mach number is ... viscous flow model", "... pass number 2, in full model" (App. II: M7 3.0590E-01 inviscid → 3.0552E-01 viscous; App. III: 1.4816E-01 → 1.4797E-01). The third pass is therefore itself iterated (at least two passes) to re-establish M8 = 1 within the `del` band under losses. The digest lists `del = 1.0E-2` (p. 32, 35) but never connects it to this outer loop; the "three-pass" architecture (§1) is under-described.
- **Geometry rescaling is exercised in App. II**, not just described: `w7=35.0` specified; output p. 43: "geometric a8 is 1.9681E+01, required is 1.9240E-01, re-scaling geometry ... scale = 9.8871E-02 ... re-scaling complete" (input coordinates effectively in inches). Digest §5 quotes App. II numbers without this context.
- **Figs. 1/4/5 geometry ≠ App. II geometry.** p. 10: "The two-dimensional test model nozzle geometry, however, is slightly different in the example case shown in Appendix II. For the general discussion of results within this section, a slightly longer two-dimensional test model nozzle geometry is used." Digest §4 and §5 present App. II numbers under the same "2-D test nozzle" label as the validation figures.
- **App. II aspect ratio `ar=1.6`** (2-D test nozzle) — governs the k-surface (sidewall) share of friction; App. II output p. 53 shows non-zero shear·area on surface k (−0.13 to −1.71 lbf) while App. III (axisymmetric, ar=1.0) has zero k contribution (p. 79). Not in digest.
- **Axis modelled as slip surface (flag −1), not symmetry (flag 0), in both examples** (App. II lines 13–16; App. III lines 15–19); j-surface contributions are identically zero in output because n̂x = 0 on r = const. Digest lists the flag semantics but not this usage.
- **Mapping step** p. 5: "Internal flow solutions from the one-dimensional stream-tube model are then mapped back to the corresponding physical geometry locations on the control volume surfaces. These results in turn are used in the evaluation of the surface integrals in Equation 12." The digest's §2.5 omits the explicit map-back from stream-tube to physical surfaces (the step that makes the per-segment integrals in Eq. 57 possible).
- **Source inconsistency on segment counts**: p. 2 "It is necessary that surfaces i and j each have the same total number of segments" vs p. 33 "It is a very good idea to make surfaces i and j with the same number of segments." Digest reports only the "required" version.
- **Source typos a reimplementer must know**: p. 6 "truncated code" (= cone); p. 10 "M9 in Equation 89 is calculated by the relation given in Equation 20" (Eq. 20 is the weight-flow function; the ideal-M9 relation is Eq. 8); Ref. 9 "NASA TM 1002847" (the digest reproduces this; the Baldwin–Barth TM number is 102847). Digest silently normalises the first and copies the third.
- **Sign convention of Eq. 12**: every term on the RHS carries a minus sign (outward normal n̂ on the CV), which is why Eq. 57 has "−λ{ + + + ... }". Digest §2.3 lists the minus signs but does not state the convention; relevant if the identity is reused as an oracle.
- **App. II/III C_f figures** (Figs. II.4 p. 40, III.4 p. 65): C_f ≈ 2–3×10⁻³; in the rocket case C_f rises along the divergent section (≈2.1→2.5×10⁻³). Available as sanity numbers for a friction comparator (C-6) but uncited.
- **Output detail useful for a per-segment bookkeeping comparator (C-2)**: App. III p. 79 per-segment pressure·area on surface i = 0, −4.120E+03 (convergent), +6.301E+02, +2.632E+02, +4.886E+01 (divergent), shear·area −3.08 … −14.0 lbf; App. II p. 53 analogous. Digest cites only the totals.
